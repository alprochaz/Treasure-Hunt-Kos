The White Gold of Tigaki

For centuries, the salt lake Alykes near Tigaki produced one of the island’s most valuable resources – salt, often called “white gold.” Traders shipped it across the Aegean, and its value was so great that in ancient times soldiers were sometimes paid with salt, giving rise to the word salary. Legend says that a wealthy merchant, who made his fortune trading this precious salt, hid his treasure somewhere in Tigaki to protect it from pirates. The clues were left behind, waiting for someone to follow them. Now it’s your turn to uncover the secret of Kos’ white gold.

Things to Do with Kids in Tigaki

A fun and easy outdoor activity for families staying in Tigaki
Looking for things to do with children in Tigaki? This self-guided treasure hunt is a great way to explore the village, the sandy beach, and the peaceful surroundings of the nearby salt lake Alykes in a playful way. The route is designed for families – easy walking, fun clues, and small discoveries along the way.
Tigaki is known for its long sandy beaches and the beautiful salt lake Alykes, once famous for producing valuable salt known as “white gold.” As you follow the clues of a legendary merchant’s hidden treasure, you’ll also discover interesting places and hidden details that many visitors simply walk past.
